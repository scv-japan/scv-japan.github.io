Hayama Symposium on Several Complex Variables

Hayama Symposium on Sevral Complex Variables will be held on December 19 - 22, 1997 at Shonan Village Center, Hayama, Kanagawa, Japan. It is financially supported by Grant-in-Aid for Scientific Research in 1997 represented by J. Noguchi. The Program Committee consists of M. Furushima (Hiroshima University), J. Noguchi (Tokyo Institute of Technology), T. Ohsawa (Nagoya University) and H. Tsuji (Tokyo Institute of Technology).

For the questions about Hayama Symposium on Several Complex Variables please mail to the e-mail address: tahensu@mis.hiroshima-u.ac.jp or contact Mikio FURUSHIMA, Integrated Arts and Science, Hiroshima University, Higashi-Hiroshima, 739, Japan (Fax: +81-824-24-0756).
