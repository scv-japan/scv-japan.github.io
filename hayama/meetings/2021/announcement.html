2021

HAYAMA Symposium on Complex Analysis in Several Variables XXII

July 22(F) – 25(Sun), 2021

will be held online in Japan Standard Time (UTC+9)

 This symposium is dedicated to the 120th anniversary of the birth of Kiyoshi Oka. 

Our regular conference site, Shonan Village Center at Hayama, is currently used as a quarantine accommodation for mild/asymptomatic cases of COVID-19. 

You may remember the 100th birthday meeting of Oka. Time flies so first!


Organizers:

Kengo Hirachi (Tokyo) Chair
Qi'an Guan (Peking: UTC+8)
Dano Kim (Seoul: UTC+9)
Shigeharu Takayama (Tokyo: UTC+9)
Finnur Larusson (Adelaide: UTC+9:30)
Yoshihiko Matsumoto (Osaka: UTC+9)

This symposium is supported by KAKENHI 20H00116
