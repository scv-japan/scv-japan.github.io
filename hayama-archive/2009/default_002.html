Sitting on a hill. Commanding a view of Mt.Fuji and overlooking Sagami Bay, Shonan Village Center offers a combination of facilities for conferences, trainings, lodging in a resort-like setting.
Shonan Village Center is the core facility of Shonan Village, which set out to send information to the world. A variety of international conferences, academic meetings and seminars have been held in this center. Our conference staff are dedicated to making your meeting successful and productive.
